# Moko demos (ATOMZ)

- **v1** (`/`): afternoon coins, trivia, staff-PIN redeem, league, owner view.
- **v2** (`/v2/`): evening-to-afternoon loop (vault a dessert, split cards, bench capsule, treat pot). Sharp editorial motion.
- **v3** (`/v3/`): grown-up lunch loop. A five-day mystery ("Lunch Story"), one chapter opened per paid lunch; a "bring a colleague" invite (friend gets 50% off lunch); coins earned only by lunch. Dark cinematic look, light and fast.

All numbers are placeholders. The 50% cap and invite rules are suggestions for the owner to confirm. Demo staff PIN: 1234.
- **v4** (`/v4/`): Moko Club. A lunch stamp keeps you in the Club for 3 days (perks switch off when it runs out); coins are separate (earn any order, lunch earns double, spend 12 to 5 PM only); a 3-reel slot and a hard "Stop at the mark" game (coins only). Same editorial look and motion as v2.
- **v5** (`/v5/`): Moko Club, redrawn from the product reels: soft rounded look, no grid lines, only transform and opacity move. Tap the stamp, type anything, press Enter and a hand stamps your card. Three game-theory style games (Beat the crowd, Slot with a bank-or-risk choice, Stop at the mark with a risk tier). Biggest discount anywhere: 25%.
- **v6** (`/v6/`): Moko Club on v2's exact look and motion. Fixes the black-screen bug (sheets and messages now really show). Tap the stamp or Bill paid, type anything, press Enter: a hand stamps the card. Cash out prints a ticket stamped USED. Three games, biggest discount 25%.
- **v7** (`/v7/`): v6 plus: the Play tab switches to v1's playful look (cards, gradient type, bouncy motion, logo companion); two simple games: Moko slots (swipe down 3 times) and Stop the number (closer = more coins, within 0.5); cleaner hand stamp; biggest discount 20%.
